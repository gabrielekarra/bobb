Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version: Leonard runs on your Mac and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. We never see your email, your screen, your documents, your questions or what Leonard writes. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The only personal data we ever hold is what you give us when you buy a license or write to support.

Who we are

The controller of the personal data described here is [legal entity name, registered address, VAT number] (“we”). Contact: privacy@leonard.app.

The app

Leonard reads the text of the window in front of you, the email you open in Mail and the drafts you write, to decide whether to help and to help. All of that is processed on your Mac by a language model running on your Mac, and stored only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Leonard. It is never transmitted to us or to a third party.

  • No account, no telemetry, no crash reporting. The app does not tell us that you use it, how, or when.
  • No network access in the engine. The component that reads your screen and runs the model cannot open network connections; this is enforced by an automated test in our build.
  • The one download. During setup, and only when you press the button, the app downloads the language model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any web server, Hugging Face receives your IP address for that request. The files are verified on your Mac against published checksums.
  • What is never read. Password managers, Keychain Access, System Settings and any app you add to the protected list are never read. Private browser windows are skipped. Secure password fields are never read in any app.
  • What is redacted. Text that looks like card numbers, private keys, API tokens, one-time codes and “password:” lines is replaced before storage.
  • Retention and deletion. Screen memory is forgotten after 30 days and decision history after 90 days by default; both are configurable. You can delete any item, any app, the last hour, today, or everything, from the Memory window and Settings.
  • Licenses are checked offline. Your license key is verified on your Mac with a public key built into the app. Nothing is sent to validate it.
  • Diagnostics are yours to send. “Export diagnostics” creates a zip on your Mac containing logs (timings and error types, never content), your settings and basic system information. Nothing is sent unless you email it to us.

This website

This website sets no cookies, loads no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers, and runs no analytics. Our hosting provider, [GitHub Pages / Cloudflare], processes standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format, by writing to privacy@leonard.app. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a change ever meant data leaving your Mac, it would be a different product, and we would say so plainly first.
