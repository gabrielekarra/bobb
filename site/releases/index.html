1.0.0 · 28 September 2026

The first release.

  • Inbox radar. Leonard reads the message you open in Mail and speaks up only when it needs you, with a reply drafted in a real Mail window. Quieter items wait in the menu bar under “For you”.
  • Ask Leonard (⌥Space). Ask about anything you've seen on screen, with sources; improve, translate, summarize, explain or reply to any selection, and replace it in place.
  • Drafts in the sender's language and tone, with one-click variants and a check that flags any date or figure the model couldn't find in the email.
  • Screen memory as text, never screenshots — searchable and deletable. Password managers and private windows are never read.
  • Adaptive quiet: a personal threshold learned from your answers, and senders you always ignore muted — every rule visible in Mind and undoable.
  • Private by architecture: the engine cannot open network connections; the model downloads once, verified.
  • English and Italian. 14-day trial. Offline license keys.
